# CubeEvil4 v1.1.2

> Keep a copy of your saves (app menu → Management → Export Player Data (.zip)).

Changes since v1.1.1.

## Fixed

- Krauser as stand-in: with Ashley along, aim + X now uses his arm (X alone still commands Ashley).

## New

- New difficulty "Berserker" above Professional (shows once Professional is unlocked): Professional with about 40% more enemies at the start of a room, enemies about 25% harder to kill (animals stay as they are) and bigger blood splatter on hits; its saves show BSK in the save list; not with the Japanese discs.
- Attaché case: press and hold an item to pick it up, drag it and let go to place it; it swaps with an item already there, like with a controller.

## Changed

- "Load shaders at start" now also prepares the shaders of health bars, costumes, other characters and extra enemies, plus many more from players' reports, so fewer stutters the first time; the first preparation takes a little longer.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

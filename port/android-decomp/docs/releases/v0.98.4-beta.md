# CubeEvil4 v0.98.4 (Beta)

> **Beta.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Savegames).

Changes since v0.98.3.

## Fixed

- Keyboards are recognised again: their keys work in the game and in the button mapping (keyboards with arrow keys were taken for a controller).
- Ashley's life gauge no longer shows gaps; infinite health during her own part had raised her life above her maximum, and saves with that are repaired.
- HUNK as the story's player: his TMP no longer vanishes from his hands after a costume change or a stock attached in the case.
- Merchant: selling something worth more than 10,000 pesetas plays his "high price" line again in every chapter.

## New

- Cheats → Player & Inventory: "Invisibility" – enemies take no notice of you and do not attack.
- Controls → Player View: a "Field of view (FoV)" slider widens or narrows the shoulder camera by up to 15 degrees.
- Display → Depth of field: a third level "Strong" next to "Light" and "Off".
- Display → Graphics → Benchmark: measures which resolution holds a steady 30 and 60 FPS at the rainy lake dock; "Dynamic" then stays at or below it on this device.

## Changed

- The FPS display now sits right at the top or bottom edge of the screen.
- The cheat page is sorted into Player & Inventory, Weapons and Game.
- Controls: touch controls and touch aiming have sections of their own, "Far camera" moved to Player View, the laser colours fill the row.
- Performance: cleaner layout, "(experimental)" dropped from the button names.
- Every menu section heading has a thin line beside it; headings, row labels, explanations and the save slot now line up with the buttons.

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames and fog banks can still flicker now and then.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Invisibility: bosses and scripted attacks still happen.
- Benchmark: only from the village part of the story (chapters 1-1 to 2-3).
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The sound can have a short gap now and then (shots, the ending movie on some devices).
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).
- Phones with only small processor cores (for example Samsung Galaxy A13): the game runs slower than normal, so cutscenes run behind their voices. Frame skip cannot help there.

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.98.4-beta.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

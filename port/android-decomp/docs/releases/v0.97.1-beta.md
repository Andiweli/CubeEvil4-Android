# CubeEvil4 v0.97.1 (Beta)

> **Beta.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Savegames).

Changes since v0.97.0.

## Fixed

- European, Japanese and US discs: the merchant now has the prices, tune-up prices and stock rules of those releases instead of the prototype's (e.g. European: first aid spray 5,000, treasure maps 3,000 / 6,000 / 9,000, TMP and mine thrower much cheaper).
- European and Japanese discs: the merchant offers first aid sprays by the healing items you carry (2, 1 or none), as in those releases.
- European, Japanese and US discs: each treasure map is only sold in its own area.
- European and Japanese discs: weapon damage as in those releases (e.g. stronger Red9, Blacktail and rifle exclusive, TMP stronger against Ganados, Regenerators tougher).
- European and Japanese discs: on Easy a slot holds more rifle rounds, shells and mines, and attache case M costs 12,000.

## Changed

- Error report: the disc check now names every file that differs from the original disc, counted by folder.

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames can still flicker now and then.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The sound can have a short gap now and then (shots, the ending movie on some devices).
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.97.1-beta.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

# CubeEvil4 v0.99.13 (Release Candidate)

> **Release Candidate.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Player Data).

Changes since v0.99.12.

## Fixed

- Wired Xbox controllers (and other wired pads Android has no layout for): LT and RT now work as L and R instead of pushing the camera to one side, and the right stick moves the camera.
- 60 FPS on phones whose screen stays at 120 Hz (OpenGL ES): each picture now asks for its own place on the screen, so every picture shows for the same time instead of short and long in turn.
- Vulkan: bright parts of the movies no longer turn green and yellow on some phones (seen on an Adreno 840), because the start-up check of the movie colours now runs under Vulkan too.

## New

- Bloom (Display → Effects; Off, Light, Strong; off by default): the brightest parts of the scene glow softly over their surroundings, while dark rooms stay dark and the HUD and menus stay sharp; the High profile turns it on only on strong devices, and it stays off while a Potato mode setting is on.

## Changed

- Random enemies: in many more rooms the ordinary Ganados now stand at new places a few metres from their own, rooms with 10 or more Ganados get one more or one fewer (20 or more: up to two), and the help says which rooms stay as they are (the village at the start of chapter 1 among them).

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames can still flicker now and then.
- Vulkan (experimental): right after installing, while the pipelines are still being built in the background, and in places the app's list does not know yet, a scene can stutter the first time it appears. Vulkan needs Android 10 or later.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Invisibility: bosses and scripted attacks still happen.
- Player characters other than Leon (Ada, HUNK, Krauser, Wesker) are not made for the main game: bugs will occur with them.
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The sound can have a short gap now and then (shots, the ending movie on some devices).
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).
- Some controllers' left stick can make Leon stutter while walking (the D-pad does not): try a higher stick range or dead zone; if it still stutters, walk with the stick for 30 seconds and send a report.
- Phones with only small processor cores (for example Samsung Galaxy A13): the game runs slower than normal, so cutscenes run behind their voices. Frame skip Normal cannot help there; Frame skip Aggressive may (not yet tested on such a phone).
- A resolution higher than the GPU manages (for example 3.5x or 4x on a Mali-G57) slows the whole game down and makes the sound crackle: use Dynamic or the level the benchmark found.
- A save that was imported with an older version and then saved again in the app keeps the mixed-up rooms (apart from the Garrador): import the original .gci again to get them back.
- HD textures: the first time a room shows them, it can stutter for a moment while they load (loading them in the background comes later).

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-0.99.13-rc.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**HD textures (optional):** the CubeEvil4 Texture Packer 1.3 comes as two files of its own with this release. Both
read your own Dolphin texture pack as a folder or a ZIP file, with DDS (BC1–BC5, BC7, uncompressed), PNG and JPG
images, and make the same `.c4tex` file. Sizes: 256 (weak devices, about 0.3 GB), 512 (recommended, about 0.7 GB) or
1024 (strong devices, about 2.3 GB); no image gets smaller than the game's own.

- Windows: `CubeEvil4-TexturePacker.exe` (no installation). Double-click it, drag the pack's folder or ZIP file into its
  window and choose a size. Copy the `.c4tex` file to the phone and import it under app menu → Cheats & Mods → Mods.
- Android: install `CubeEvil4-TexturePacker-1.3.apk` (Android 7 or later), choose the pack's ZIP file or folder and a
  size, then Start (a phone needs far longer than a PC: keep it on the charger). The file is saved in the Download
  folder; "Import into CubeEvil4" hands it straight to CubeEvil4.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

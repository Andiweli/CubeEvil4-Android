# CubeEvil4 v0.98.2 (Beta)

> **Beta.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Savegames).

Changes since v0.98.1.

## Fixed

- Instant save is no longer refused in whole rooms: in boss rooms and scripted rooms it now works before the fight starts and after it is over.
- Instant save now also works in the village hall with the boulder, on the lake shore and at the mine lift.
- Instant save now also works in Ashley's own section and with Ada, HUNK, Krauser or Wesker as the story's player.
- Retail discs: the small and medium money chests in the "You got ... PTAS." window are shown as large as in the original release again.

## New

- Android Automotive: CubeEvil4 runs on cars with Android built in (parked only). The car's system bars no longer cover the picture, and the game sound follows the car's audio focus.
- Graphics → Picture: "Lens flares" (off by default) gives candles, torches and lamps a subtle camera lens flare: a soft starburst at the light and faint coloured rings towards the picture's centre. It stays steady, fades in and out smoothly and never shines through walls.
- Graphics → Picture: "Minimap" (off by default) shows the current room at the bottom left, with its walls, pillars and closed doors, turning with you so the way you face is always up.
- Controls → Laser Sight: the laser beam and its dot can be red (default), blue, green or yellow.

## Changed

- Switching between day and night mode (on cars, or the dark-mode schedule on phones) no longer restarts the game screen.
- Instant save stays locked only while a boss fight, a chase or a timed escape is running; the app menu says so.
- CubeEvil4 now needs Android 7.0 or later (Google Play requires it).
- App menu → Display: the picture format comes first, depth of field, lens flares and the two picture filters are under "Post-processing", the minimap and the FPS display (before under Performance) under "Optional features".

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames can still flicker now and then.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The sound can have a short gap now and then (shots, the ending movie on some devices).
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.98.2-beta.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

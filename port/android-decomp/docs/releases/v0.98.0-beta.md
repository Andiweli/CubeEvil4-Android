# CubeEvil4 v0.98.0 (Beta)

> **Beta.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Savegames).

Changes since v0.97.1.

## Fixed

- Invert vertical: a change in the game's own options (Controller, "reverse") is kept and no longer reset at the next start.
- Retro pixel filter: fine textures on the ground no longer flicker when the camera moves.
- European, Japanese and US discs: Leon's aiming stance and Ada's camera as in those releases.
- The game no longer crashes when you close the attache case after changing weapons in rooms with many lights (e.g. Leon in the castle, Ada in The Mercenaries' village barn).

## New

- Cheats: new "Player character" section – play the story as Ada, HUNK, Krauser or Wesker instead of Leon; the change happens at once (the room loads again where you stand).
- Cheats: "Special costumes" (Leon and Ashley only) – Leon and Ashley wear their special costumes (Ada as Leon's stand-in her second outfit), switched on or off at once.
- Saves made with another character still hold Leon, so they load with whoever is chosen in the menu; cutscenes and radio calls keep showing Leon.

## Changed

- Touch controls: every button has a dark olive border (RetroTouch 1.0.0-beta.9).

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames can still flicker now and then.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The sound can have a short gap now and then (shots, the ending movie on some devices).
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.98.0-beta.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

# CubeEvil4 v1.1.3

> Keep a copy of your saves (app menu → Management → Export Player Data (.zip)).

Changes since v1.1.2.

## Fixed

- Android 7 to 8.1: the app no longer closes right after the start (seen on an NVIDIA Shield Tablet); also fixed a close with "Force controller detection" on Android 7 to 11 and with controller vibration on Android 12.
- The mouse camera is no longer much slower with fast gaming mice (1000 Hz and more), so turning and looking feel the same on every mouse.
- "Stack items" switched off or set lower: picking up ammo no longer cuts a larger stack down to the normal maximum.
- Vulkan: the texture filter (anisotropic filtering) offers up to 16× (or the device's maximum) again; before, it could not be set above the level chosen last.
- No more false "hang" entries in error reports while the shaders are prepared at start (or photo mode is open).

## New

- "Turn speed" slider next to "Turn Leon with mouse / look area" (0.25× to 4×): makes Leon's turn by mouse or look area faster or slower.
- Cheat "Bloody mode": blood pools from hits and fallen enemies stay on the floor until you leave the room (up to 160 at once, the oldest go first).
- Cheats "All weapons at the merchant" and "All tune-ups at the merchant": the merchant sells every weapon and offers every tune-up level, exclusives included, at his normal prices; your save keeps what you buy, not the offer.

## Changed

- The cheat "Stack ammo" is now "Stack items": grenades, herbs and their mixes, first aid sprays, fish and eggs stack too (up to 250, 500 or 999); mixing a herb from a stack puts the mix onto its stack or into a free place in the case.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

# CubeEvil4 v0.95.1 (Beta)

> **Beta.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Savegames).

Changes since v0.95.0.

## Fixed

- Picture format "4:3": in play the game no longer cuts the picture at the old letterbox rows.
- European discs: the "EMPTY" sign of the ammo counter stays in the game language instead of cycling through all five.
- Inventory: Leon without his jacket wears the knife on his chest strap, as in the original.
- Sniper scope: at resolution 1× the horizontal line of the crosshair is visible.
- Sound: less crackling and a clearer sound.

## New

- Controls: every action can be mapped to a controller or keyboard key, actions of two buttons stay combinations.
- Mouse: the game captures the mouse (Android 8 and newer, F1 releases it), with a sensitivity slider.
- Cheats: "Automatic QTEs" and "Unlock extra games" (Assignment Ada, The Mercenaries).

## Changed

- Picture format "4:3" now shows a true 4:3 picture over the full height.
- Touch aiming with the touch area follows the finger and stops when the finger stops.
- Phones with two fast cores: the graphics work is spread better over the cores.
- In-game menu: rows of three buttons line up under the rows of two.

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames can still flicker now and then.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.95.1-beta.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

# CubeEvil4 v1.1.0

> Keep a copy of your saves (app menu → Management → Export Player Data (.zip)).

Changes since v1.0.0.

## Fixed

- Vibration did nothing on many devices and controllers: the game's own vibration switch was off in most save files; it now follows the app menu's "Vibration" (on unless set to Off), and switching it in the game's options changes the app's setting too.
- FPS drops on phones with only two fast processor cores (for example Helio G85 or G81) when "Performance cores" was on from the start: the game's helper threads now move to the slower cores as intended (tried again for 30 s when Android does not allow it at first), so the two fast cores stay free for the game and its pictures.
- A crash at the end of the game with the European discs (the escape, room r333), when the timer ran out or Leon died there: a log check of the app read a broken address; it now leaves such addresses alone.
- Uneven 60 FPS with Vulkan on screens faster than 60 Hz (for example 120 Hz): the counter showed 60, but many pictures came a refresh early or late; each picture now asks the screen for its own moment, as OpenGL ES already did, so 60 FPS look even (follows "Even frame pacing"; Vulkan drivers without this stay as before).
- Real-time shadows with Vulkan: on some phones (for example with Adreno 8xx graphics) characters could get stripes of their own shadow and of nearby objects' shadows on their body; Vulkan now marks the characters exactly as OpenGL ES does.
- The "Mouse Sensitivity" label wrapped onto two lines in some languages (for example German); its slider now starts under the "Device" buttons and the label keeps one line.
- Phones whose small processor cores reach a higher clock than their big ones (for example Snapdragon 460): "Performance cores" (on in every performance profile) put the game on the small cores; the app now asks Android which cores are the strong ones.
- Shooting gallery: after three of a game's bottle caps the second, harder round now comes as in the finished game; the sixth (special) cap is now awarded (4,000 points or more, or more than 24 hits without hitting Ashley's target); the bonus for a complete set counts the right six caps; leftover debug keys of the prototype (X ended a round at once, X on an empty case gave its cap) are gone.
- "Save report" and "Send report" could make the app stop responding on slow phones with a large shader cache (for example after an update over the version before without clearing its data); the report is now made in the background.
- Thin lines (the attaché case's grid, the frames around its items, the laser sight's beam) on phones whose graphics chip draws lines only 1 pixel wide (seen on an Adreno 650 with Vulkan): above 1× resolution they were much thinner than the game means, and with an HD texture pack's darker case floor some grid lines disappeared; they are now drawn as wide as the game asks.

## New

- Ashley stuck somewhere? Press her button (Wait / Follow) twice quickly and she fades in right behind Leon and follows him; a single press works as before.
- Gyro aiming with a controller's own gyroscope (for example DualSense or DualShock 4, Android 12 and later): Controls → "Gyro from" Auto / Device / Controller; it still aims only while you raise a weapon.
- Enemy health bars (Cheats & Mods → Enemies and world, off by default): a thin bar above each enemy shows its health, green, yellow when low, red when nearly dead, orange while a Plaga is out of its head; it stays centred above the head (also when a weapon is raised) and shows only when you can see the enemy, not through walls or closed doors; none on the merchant, allies and animals; hidden in cutscenes and menus.
- Cheat "More enemies" (Cheats & Mods → Enemies and world, off by default): about 40 percent more of a room's ordinary Ganados at random places, on every difficulty; bosses, special enemies and waves stay as they are; story only.
- Mouse in the title and options menus: moving the mouse moves the highlight, a left click chooses (idea from a user's ModernTPS mod).
- Option "Reload without aiming" (Controls → Button mapping with "Keyboard & Mouse", under "Device", off by default): the reload key alone (R) reloads without raising the weapon (idea from a user's ModernTPS mod).
- Option "Skip intro" (Display → Optional features, off by default): the game starts straight at its title menu, without the logos and the health warning; the menu fades in.
- Option "Photo mode" (Display → Optional features, off by default): L3 (P on a keyboard, the "Photo mode" button at the top of the app menu on touch) freezes the game and frees the camera, which moves through the whole room and stops at walls and floors; hide the HUD, Leon, Ashley and the enemies one by one; L and R switch camera effects (wide angle, telephoto, dolly zoom, Dutch angle, orbit); SELECT opens the app menu for filters and effects while the game stays frozen; B, L3 or Esc goes back to the game as it was. Take the picture with your device's screenshot.
- Option "Load shaders at start" (Management, next to "Clear shader cache", off by default): each start first prepares every known shader with a progress screen, so rooms and effects do not stutter the first time; the first time takes about a minute with Vulkan and up to several minutes with OpenGL ES on slow phones, later seconds; it cannot be skipped; needs 4 GB RAM or more (greyed out below).
- Option "Display resolution" (Display, Native by default): Native, 1080p, 900p or 720p sent to the screen, which stretches it; faster on phones with very sharp screens, above all with CRT, bloom, FXAA or Sharpen (idea from the RE4Wii-Android port).
- Option "Upscaler" (Display, Off by default): when the game's resolution is lower than the screen's, Qualcomm's SGSR (light, recommended) or AMD's FSR 1 (sharpest, about twice the work) enlarges the picture instead of the plain soft stretch; OpenGL ES and Vulkan; not with the CRT filter.
- Frame skip "Auto" (Performance → Frame skip, an arrow cell: Off / Auto / Normal / Aggressive): nothing is left out while the game runs at full speed; only when it stays under 30 pictures a second for about half a second does the renderer's frame skip help, and only when it stays under 24 for two seconds the game's own too, more gently than Aggressive (a picture only once the game is a whole frame behind, so a processor that is only a little too slow keeps its pictures smooth); off again after two seconds at full speed; not with 60 FPS.
- Option "Force controller detection" (Controls → Button mapping with "Controller", under "Device", off by default): every key of an external device counts as a controller button, for gamepads Android does not detect or lists as a keyboard; assign them above. A real keyboard then counts as a controller too (idea from a fork).
- Cheat "Stack ammo" (Cheats & Mods → General and inventory → Stacking, Standard / 250 / 500 / 999, Standard by default): ammo stacks up to 250, 500 or 999 in one slot; grenades, recovery items and treasures stay as in the game; a smaller choice later keeps larger stacks, they are used up one by one.
- Cheat "Refill ammo" (Cheats & Mods → General and inventory, beside "Stack ammo"): every ammo stack in the attaché case is filled up to the "Stack ammo" value (Standard: its type's normal maximum); no new ammo, the weapons' loaded rounds stay.
- Option "Even frame pacing" (Performance, on by default): off shows each picture as soon as it is ready, as in older versions (a little more direct, less even); on keeps every picture on the screen equally long.

## Changed

- More memory for a room's enemies: the game's room memory where the enemy kinds load is about twice as large (14.5 → 29.5 MB), so rooms with "More enemies", "Random enemies" or many enemy kinds less often fall back to the slower way (idea from a fork).
- Screens faster than 60 Hz with 60 FPS on: the picture thread now asks Android for the speed of 60 pictures a second instead of 30, and the 60 Hz request is repeated when you come back to the game or the screen changes (idea from a fork).
- Phones that hold their fastest processor core back while they warm up (for example some Xiaomi phones, even when Android reports no heat): with "Performance cores" on, the picture thread now moves to the cores that may run faster at the moment, and back when the fast core is free again; the error log lists every core's clocks once a minute.
- "Source and updates" (the link to the official page) is now also at the bottom of the app menu's start page, under the game language.
- Enemy health bars now appear only within about 15 m and fade in and out smoothly (0.4 s) instead of popping up, also when an enemy steps behind a wall or a door or dies.
- Cheats & Mods: "Random items" and "Random enemies" moved to "Enemies and world", as its first row.
- Distant floors and walls shimmer less: room textures that come without smaller versions (about 3,000 of them) now get them when a room loads, made the way the game's own are; foliage and fences keep their thickness in the distance. Up close nothing changes; it costs about 2 MB of graphics memory per room.
- With anti-aliasing (MSAA) on, the edges of foliage, fences and hair are smoothed too; without MSAA nothing changes.
- Performance profile "Low": frame skip "Auto" and the view distance "Short" (before Medium; never "None"), for phones whose processor is the limit (for example the cable car on a Snapdragon 460); if "Low" was active and unchanged, the update takes the new values by itself (once); if you changed a setting after choosing "Low", your own values stay.
- App menu: every choice of more than two values (frame skip, view distance, picture format, resolution, anti-aliasing, upscaler, display resolution, texture filter, depth of field, lens flares, bloom, real-time shadows, sharpen, CRT, colour, FPS display, particles, touch controls, button style, gyro from, touch aiming, laser colour, vibration, game language, costumes) is now one cell with ‹ and ›: tap an arrow or the middle; dots show the steps; the cell turns golden whenever its value is not Off (or None).
- App menu: sliders are cells of the same kind, side by side (for example "Stick dead zone" and "Stick range"): name small on top, value big, the bar inside; tap ‹ or › for one step or drag the bar. Switches show their name small on top and On / Off big below (golden when On); all cells and buttons are equally high.
- App menu with a controller or keyboard: the D-pad (arrow keys) always moves between the cells, A (Enter) steps a cell forward, L1 / R1 (Q / E or Page up / Page down) change its value back and forth; the line at the bottom says so.
- App menu: the game language changes only when you confirm it (tap the middle or press A); the arrows and L1 / R1 just show the next language, and leaving the cell without confirming keeps the current one.
- App menu: related settings sit side by side: display resolution and upscaler, "MSAA anti-aliasing" and "FXAA anti-aliasing", texture filter and sharper menu text, "GameCube TV filter" (before "Original GameCube TV filter") and retro pixel filter, minimap and photo mode (the Information HUD with "Skip intro" under them), touch controls and button style, "Turn Leon with mouse / look area" and gentler QTEs; the HD textures' "Sharpness" is golden at "Full".
- App menu: the start page shows the app's icon with the title "CubeEvil4 Options".
- The FPS display also shows the pictures on the screen whenever a frame skip leaves some out (before, only the game's own 30 a second were shown, also when far fewer pictures reached the screen).
- App menu: every page (Display, Audio, Controls, Performance, Cheats & Mods) now has short labels on the left beside its groups (for example "Resolution", "Filtering", "Gyroscope", "Assists", "Stick options", "Basic weapons", "Mercenaries"), always two cells a row; Display's last section is called "Optional"; on Controls the laser sight sits under the view, "Gyro from" under gyro aiming, and the button mapping has a section of its own; on Performance "Save bandwidth (Vulkan)" sits beside "Even frame pacing", the two options Vulkan greys out under them; on Cheats & Mods "Main character" (Leon) and "Mercenaries" (Ada, HUNK, Krauser, Wesker), the texture pack's Import and Delete side by side.

# CubeEvil4 v0.96.8 (Beta)

> **Beta.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Savegames).

Changes since v0.96.7.

## Fixed

- Cheats: the Mine Thrower button now really adds the Mine Thrower, with its scope attached.
- Castle: a crash when entering a room with some saves (seen with US discs) no longer happens.
- Examining a weapon after its exclusive tune-up shows the exclusive's own text, as in the retail game.
- The Mine Thrower's firepower no longer reads 0.0 after its exclusive tune-up.
- Castle, the two El Gigantes over the lava: loading an instant save made during that fight no longer crashes.

## Changed

- Instant save is now also refused in the rooms of the El Gigante fights (village and castle) and of U-3, the village hall, the besieged cabin with Luis and the sniper sequence covering Ashley.

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames can still flicker now and then.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The sound can have a short gap now and then (shots, the ending movie on some devices).
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.96.8-beta.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

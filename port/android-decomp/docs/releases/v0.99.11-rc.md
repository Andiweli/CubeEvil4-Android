# CubeEvil4 v0.99.11 (Release Candidate)

> **Release Candidate.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Player Data).

Changes since v0.99.10.

## Fixed

- Ada as the story's character: after reloading the fully upgraded TMP she no longer stays bent forward; the Punisher and the semi-automatic rifle no longer bend her while she reloads them at higher reload speeds (her own reload motion at every level).
- Wesker as the story's character: the case screen no longer shows a silencer on his handgun when it has none.
- App menu with a controller: the focused button is now clearly golden on every phone, also on sub-pages, sliders, the profile and benchmark buttons and in all pop-ups (on some phones, for example Xiaomi with dark mode, it was only a faint dark frame).

## New

- Information HUD (Display → Optional features, instead of the FPS display, at the same places): check boxes choose what it shows – FPS of the game and the screen, display Hz, resolution and the play time (H:M:S). The play time is the game's own, which every save keeps (typewriter and instant save); time in the menu or in the background does not count.

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames can still flicker now and then.
- Vulkan (experimental): right after installing, while the pipelines are still being built in the background, and in places the app's list does not know yet, a scene can stutter the first time it appears. Vulkan needs Android 10 or later.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Invisibility: bosses and scripted attacks still happen.
- Player characters other than Leon (Ada, HUNK, Krauser, Wesker) are not made for the main game: bugs will occur with them.
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The sound can have a short gap now and then (shots, the ending movie on some devices).
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).
- Some controllers' left stick can make Leon stutter while walking (the D-pad does not): try a higher stick range or dead zone; if it still stutters, walk with the stick for 30 seconds and send a report.
- Phones with only small processor cores (for example Samsung Galaxy A13): the game runs slower than normal, so cutscenes run behind their voices. Frame skip Normal cannot help there; Frame skip Aggressive may (not yet tested on such a phone).
- A resolution higher than the GPU manages (for example 3.5x or 4x on a Mali-G57) slows the whole game down and makes the sound crackle: use Dynamic or the level the benchmark found.
- A save that was imported with an older version and then saved again in the app keeps the mixed-up rooms (apart from the Garrador): import the original .gci again to get them back.

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-0.99.11-rc.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

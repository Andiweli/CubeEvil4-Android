# CubeEvil4 v0.99.10 (Release Candidate)

> **Release Candidate.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Player Data).

Changes since v0.99.9.

## Fixed

- The app menu, its pages and its questions no longer bring up Android's navigation bar and status bar; the game stays full screen (a swipe from the edge still shows them for a moment).
- Picture format "Native" on wide screens (for example 20:9 phones): the binoculars now cover the sides in black instead of showing the game world beside them, like the rifle scope; this also applies to the binocular views in cutscenes.
- Picture format "4:3": texts in menu boxes (files, merchant, item names and descriptions, map) no longer start over the boxes' left edges; the whole picture now keeps one shape, as in "4:3 (GC)" but over the full height.
- Vulkan: with the CRT filter the picture no longer flashes bright around the edges now and then (each time Dynamic resolution changed its level); the colour and PSone filters no longer drop out for a moment either.
- The pictures of a GX dump series (R3) no longer show the minimap over most of the picture.

## New

- Performance profiles on the menu's start page: Custom, Low, Medium and High in one row with the Benchmark button, made for this device from the benchmark and its data (graphics chip, processor, memory, display); the suggested one is marked, and every profile uses the performance cores. Changing one of their settings by hand makes it Custom, and Custom is kept while you try a profile.
- On the very first start the app measures the device once the title menu shows (about 2–3 minutes, B / Back cancels), suggests a profile – on strong devices with anti-aliasing, FXAA or a texture filter – and checks it with a second, short measurement before you accept it; if the suggestion runs less smoothly than your settings, it says so. After an update the menu asks once whether to measure now.
- Krauser as the story's character (Cheats → Player character) now has his bow: every room start gives it back and fills his arrows up to 40 where the case has room (Infinite ammo keeps them full). With Leon or another character the bow and arrows leave the case, and saves on the memory card never keep them.
- Wesker as the story's character now has his silencer: every room start gives it back and puts it on the handgun, where the case has room. With Leon or another character it leaves the case (the pistol stays), and saves on the memory card never keep it.

## Changed

- The benchmark (start page or Display → Graphics) now measures with your own look settings (depth of field, lens flares, sharpening, filters) and with the performance cores on (also in both windows of its check), always with Leon (whichever character the cheat menu chose), and suggests a whole profile instead of only setting the resolution; "Ignore" keeps the old behaviour (Dynamic up to the measured levels).

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames can still flicker now and then.
- Vulkan (experimental): right after installing, while the pipelines are still being built in the background, and in places the app's list does not know yet, a scene can stutter the first time it appears. Vulkan needs Android 10 or later.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Invisibility: bosses and scripted attacks still happen.
- Player characters other than Leon (Ada, HUNK, Krauser, Wesker) are not made for the main game: bugs will occur with them.
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The sound can have a short gap now and then (shots, the ending movie on some devices).
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).
- Some controllers' left stick can make Leon stutter while walking (the D-pad does not): try a higher stick range or dead zone; if it still stutters, walk with the stick for 30 seconds and send a report.
- Phones with only small processor cores (for example Samsung Galaxy A13): the game runs slower than normal, so cutscenes run behind their voices. Frame skip Normal cannot help there; Frame skip Aggressive may (not yet tested on such a phone).
- A resolution higher than the GPU manages (for example 3.5x or 4x on a Mali-G57) slows the whole game down and makes the sound crackle: use Dynamic or the level the benchmark found.
- A save that was imported with an older version and then saved again in the app keeps the mixed-up rooms (apart from the Garrador): import the original .gci again to get them back.

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-0.99.10-rc.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

# CubeEvil4 v1.1.1

> Keep a copy of your saves (app menu → Management → Export Player Data (.zip)).

Changes since v1.1.0.

## Fixed

- Touch controls "Suppress" did not hide the on-screen buttons on a Chromebook with a touchscreen (or with a keyboard or mouse but no gamepad): with "Suppress" a keyboard, a mouse or a Chromebook now count like a controller, so the buttons stay hidden; "Auto" is unchanged.
- With MSAA anti-aliasing (2× or 4×) and OpenGL ES, grass, hair, fire, the HUD and other cut-out parts could disappear on some phones (seen on an Adreno 730): OpenGL ES uses the plain alpha test with MSAA again, as in 1.0.0; the smoother cut-out edges of 1.1.0 stay with Vulkan.

## Changed

- Performance cores "Default": Android now also learns how long each picture may take and how long it took (its performance hint, Android 13 and later), as with "Use performance cores" before; the cores stay Android's choice, but phones that kept the picture thread on slow clocks (for example with MediaTek chips, at 60 FPS) can now raise them.
- "Use performance cores": the picture thread and the game thread are put back on their fast cores within one picture when the phone moves them away (before: about once a second; on a vivo with a Dimensity 9200 they spent most of the time on the slower cores).

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

# CubeEvil4 v0.96.0 (Beta)

> **Beta.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Savegames).

Changes since v0.95.2.

## Fixed

- Handcannon: Ganados with a chainsaw no longer take several shots.
- European discs on Easy: you start with the shotgun, as in the retail game.
- 60 FPS: no more ghost images when new particles appear (colour puzzle, fire, dissolving enemies).
- Japanese and Korean discs: the opening movie's subtitles show the right characters.

## New

- Audio page in the app menu: separate sliders for the music and the sound effects.
- Cheats have a page of their own in the app menu.
- Potato mode (Performance page): switch off weather or special effects for more speed on weaker devices.
- Save import: European saves, whole Dolphin memory cards (.raw, .gcp) and ZIP files.
- Aiming by touch or controller: adjustable aim speed and vertical inversion.
- Update button in the app menu: shows when a newer version is out and installs it.

## Changed

- App menu order: Display, Audio, Controls, Performance, Cheats, Management.
- Controls page: motion controls first, the button mapping in a section of its own.
- Performance page: the picture output options (FPS display, frame skip, 60 FPS) are now under "Experimental".

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames can still flicker now and then.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The sound can have a short gap now and then (shots, the ending movie on some devices).
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.96.0-beta.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

# CubeEvil4 v0.95.2 (Beta)

> **Beta.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Savegames).

Changes since v0.95.1.

## Fixed

- European discs: the way past the catapults towards the clock tower is no longer blocked.
- European discs: the armor statues in Ashley's section leave no invisible walls behind once the knights come alive.
- Handcannon: the merchant shows its real firepower, and the exclusive upgrade gives endless ammo.

## New

- Japanese discs (biohazard4, G4BJ08) can be imported and play in Japanese.
- The Korean fan translation of the Japanese discs can be imported and plays in Korean.

## Changed

- Fewer stutters the first time an effect appears: 168 more shaders are prepared ahead.
- Error report: sound output dropouts are now recorded, to find the occasional gap in the shot sound.

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames can still flicker now and then.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The shot sound can have a short gap now and then.
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.95.2-beta.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

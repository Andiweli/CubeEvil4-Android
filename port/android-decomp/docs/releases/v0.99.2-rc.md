# CubeEvil4 v0.99.2 (Release Candidate)

> **Release Candidate.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Player Data).

Changes since v0.99.1.

## Fixed

- European, Japanese and Korean discs: after the two El Gigantes in the lava hall, the open iron gate no longer offers "Check" with the "It's locked" message.
- Picture format "4:3": in the menus (inventory, files, map) the life gauge is no longer cut off by the game's black bars.

## New

- Export Player Data (formerly Export Savegames) also saves your own touch layout, and Import Player Data brings it back (also from a ZIP, asking before it replaces yours, in use right away).
- Stick dead zone and stick range (app menu → Controls → Controller, Keyboard and Mouse): for sticks that drift or do not reach their edge; the defaults change nothing.
- Vibration in the controller (app menu → Controls → Vibration: Auto, Device, Controller, Off): Auto uses a connected controller's own motors (Android 12 and later), handhelds keep their own vibration.
- Random items (app menu → Cheats → Game): the ammo, herbs, grenades, pesetas and treasures lying in the story's rooms are shuffled; keys, weapons and story items stay, so you never get stuck.
- Sharpen (app menu → Display → Filter: Off, Light, Strong): brings back the texture detail that the wide picture formats soften when they enlarge the picture; works with the other filters, movies stay as they are.

## Changed

- Ashley's skirt reaction only comes when Leon looks up at her (aiming or turning the camera up), as in the retail releases, no longer when he just stands below her.

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames and fog banks can still flicker now and then.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Invisibility: bosses and scripted attacks still happen.
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The sound can have a short gap now and then (shots, the ending movie on some devices).
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).
- Some controllers' left stick can make Leon stutter while walking (the D-pad does not): try a higher stick range or dead zone; if it still stutters, walk with the stick for 30 seconds and send a report.
- Phones with only small processor cores (for example Samsung Galaxy A13): the game runs slower than normal, so cutscenes run behind their voices. Frame skip cannot help there.

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-0.99.2-rc.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

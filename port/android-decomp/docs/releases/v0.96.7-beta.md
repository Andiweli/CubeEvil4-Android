# CubeEvil4 v0.96.7 (Beta)

> **Beta.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Savegames).

Changes since v0.96.6.

## Fixed

- Island, U-3 in the cages: killing it after a retry from a checkpoint no longer leaves the game stuck; the shutter lifts and the way on opens.
- Some phones crashed after hours of play, in the laboratory or after a while in the map, shop or case: the game no longer keeps every shader it ever made in memory, only those of the current area.
- Auto aim no longer points the laser above the enemy, also with gyro aiming: it aims at the enemy and a slightly shaking hand no longer breaks it.
- The Mercenaries, Krauser: his arm glows in the intro and stays usable, cancelling it makes no grunt, and the intro keeps moving while his hint shows.
- The Mercenaries, Krauser's bow: no "0" in the case, and with retail discs the arrow icon in the weapon circle.
- Chainsaw deaths: Krauser's head now falls off, and Ada loses her real head instead of a white one.
- A knee kick that does not kill now makes its hit sound.
- Ada's camera sits a little further back, as in the retail game.
- Grabbed while zoomed in with the rifle: the camera goes back behind the player at once instead of looking into the sky.
- Aim and fire held while closing the case: the gun now fires, not only aims.

## New

- Controls: "Far camera", the shoulder view a little further behind Leon while walking and standing; aiming and tight spots as before, with smooth moves. Off by default.
- Display: "Original TV filter", the GameCube's own vertical softening of the picture for the TV; off by default.
- Performance > Experimental: "Vertex ring buffer (experimental, for Mali)", off by default; may save some time on Mali GPUs.
- Cheats: Handcannon, Chicago Typewriter, Rocket Launcher, Mine Thrower (with scope) and Matilda, each fully upgraded.

## Changed

- The error report keeps the logs of the last crash until the next one, also when the app was started again in between.
- The error log notes how long loading the game's data takes, to find the cause of stutters.
- Cheats: the Killer7 button is just "Killer7"; the weapons sit right below the switches.

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames can still flicker now and then.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The sound can have a short gap now and then (shots, the ending movie on some devices).
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.96.7-beta.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

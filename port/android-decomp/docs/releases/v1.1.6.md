# CubeEvil4 v1.1.6

> Keep a copy of your saves (app menu → Management → Export Player Data (.zip)).

Changes since v1.1.5.

## New

- Rapid fire (Controls → Assists, off by default): every gun fires twice as fast, for every playable character.
- Minimap: the room's treasures (gold), items (blue) and blue medallions (Display → Optional → Treasures and items; "With hidden" also shows what waits in closed chests, crates and enemies; off by default).
- Move and aim (Controls → Stick options, off by default): while you aim, the left stick walks your character forward, back and to the sides, and you keep aiming with the right stick, touch or mouse. The legs really walk while the upper body keeps the aim (Leon, Ada, HUNK, Krauser, Wesker).
- Controller aim with: Left Stick (as on the GameCube) or Right Stick.
- Language packs for the European discs: import a fan translation (folder or ZIP) under Cheats & Mods → Language pack, then pick it under Game language. CubeEvil4 ships and links no packs.

## Changed

- Controls page: "Touch aim" is now the "Aiming" section with "Touch aim with" and "Controller aim with"; the controller, keyboard and mouse section follows right after it, with Move and aim next to Vibration.

## Fixed

- Gyro aiming: up/down and left/right no longer swapped on devices whose motion sensor is built in turned (e.g. Anbernic RG477M).

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

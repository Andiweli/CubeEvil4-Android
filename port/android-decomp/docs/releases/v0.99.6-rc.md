# CubeEvil4 v0.99.6 (Release Candidate)

> **Release Candidate.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Player Data).

Changes since v0.99.5.

## Fixed

- Lens flares: no more glowing star on the merchant's face before the cabin; halos now only appear at the room's lights (torches, fires, lamps, candles), never on characters.
- Performance → Potato mode: "Particle effects" and "Shadows & light" now really work in the game (since 0.99.3 the setting never reached it).
- A rare crash at a loading screen or when the end credits start (the game closed by itself).
- Playing the story as Ada, HUNK, Krauser or Wesker: no more crash when dying or choosing Retry after changing weapons.
- Automatic QTEs: the pause menu no longer gets A pressed by itself after pausing during a quick-time prompt (the Yes/No question kept opening and closing).
- The operating room before the last boss: the monitors show the X-ray of the chest and the parasite again instead of only green.
- Vulkan: quitting the game or Android closing it in the background no longer switches the next start to OpenGL ES with the message that Vulkan crashed; only a real crash while Vulkan starts does.

## New

- Performance → Potato mode: "View distance" (Normal, Medium, Short) brings the distance fog nearer and leaves out what lies beyond it (from the OUYA version). It helps slower phones at a low resolution (outdoors up to about half of the objects); at high resolutions it gains little.

## Changed

- Display → Effects: "Lens flares" has three steps: Off, Lens flares (only the coloured rings), Halos + lens flares (also the glow and star at the lights, as before; your earlier "On" becomes this).
- The app menu's Update button now opens the newest release's page on GitHub: download and install the APK there (no more Play Protect warning; the app no longer asks to install apps).
- Performance → Potato mode: "Particle effects" stays lit at "Less" and is only off at "None".
- Performance with Vulkan: "Merge draw calls" and "Vertex ring buffer" are greyed out; Vulkan does not need them (they still work with OpenGL ES).

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames can still flicker now and then.
- Vulkan (experimental): right after installing, while the pipelines are still being built in the background, and in places the app's list does not know yet, a scene can stutter the first time it appears. Vulkan needs Android 10 or later.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Invisibility: bosses and scripted attacks still happen.
- Player characters other than Leon (Ada, HUNK, Krauser, Wesker) are not made for the main game: bugs will occur with them.
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The sound can have a short gap now and then (shots, the ending movie on some devices).
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).
- Some controllers' left stick can make Leon stutter while walking (the D-pad does not): try a higher stick range or dead zone; if it still stutters, walk with the stick for 30 seconds and send a report.
- Phones with only small processor cores (for example Samsung Galaxy A13): the game runs slower than normal, so cutscenes run behind their voices. Frame skip cannot help there.
- A resolution higher than the GPU manages (for example 3.5x or 4x on a Mali-G57) slows the whole game down and makes the sound crackle: use Dynamic or the level the benchmark found.

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-0.99.6-rc.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

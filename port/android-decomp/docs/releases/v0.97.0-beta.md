# CubeEvil4 v0.97.0 (Beta)

> **Beta.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Savegames).

Changes since v0.96.9.

## New

- Graphics: a new section "Filter" at the end of the graphics page with the "Retro pixel filter", which draws the game as a PlayStation would have (wobbly polygons, textures without perspective correction and filtering, a coarse 320-pixel picture with 15-bit colour and dithering).

## Fixed

- Instant save: Ashley hidden in a box or closet is back in her hiding place after loading.
- The Mercenaries: when Ada is beheaded, her hair no longer stays on the falling body.
- European discs on Easy: the camera follows Ashley again when you play as her, instead of switching to fixed angles.
- U-3 fight (container yard): the game no longer freezes and Leon and U-3 no longer vanish when U-3 hides on the containers with almost no health left.

## Changed

- Graphics: the TV filter is now called "Original GameCube TV filter" and sits in the new "Filter" section.

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames can still flicker now and then.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The sound can have a short gap now and then (shots, the ending movie on some devices).
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.97.0-beta.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

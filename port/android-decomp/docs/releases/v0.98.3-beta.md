# CubeEvil4 v0.98.3 (Beta)

> **Beta.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Savegames).

Changes since v0.98.2.

## Fixed

- Krauser as the story's player: his kick is his double kick again and knocks the Ganado down, and his knee kick is his own, no longer Wesker's palm strike.
- Ada as the story's player: her kick and the Ganados' grabs use her own animations, as in The Mercenaries.
- HUNK as the story's player: the TMP with the stock attached no longer vanishes from his hands.
- HUNK as the story's player: the rifle scope and the binoculars now look from his eyes, no longer from his hips.
- Ada, HUNK, Krauser or Wesker as the story's player: the picture no longer goes dark when Ashley is told to wait; the rooms' camera angles and lighting are now the same as with Leon.
- Ada, HUNK, Krauser or Wesker as the story's player: Leon is no longer pitch black in some cutscenes.
- Ada as the story's player: Leon no longer wears his first jacket over his later outfit in cutscenes.
- The FPS display keeps clear of rounded screen corners and the camera cutout, so it is no longer cut off.
- The minimap now sits at the bottom left of the picture in every picture format, opposite the life and ammo gauge; with "4:3" and "4:3 (GC)" it was too far left or below the picture.

## Changed

- App menu → Display: the FPS display button now moves it on with each press: bottom left, bottom centre, top left, top centre, off; it no longer has to cover the minimap.

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames can still flicker now and then.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The sound can have a short gap now and then (shots, the ending movie on some devices).
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).
- Phones with only small processor cores (for example Samsung Galaxy A13): the game runs slower than normal, so cutscenes run behind their voices. Frame skip cannot help there.

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.98.3-beta.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

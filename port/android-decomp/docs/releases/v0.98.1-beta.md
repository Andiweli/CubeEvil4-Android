# CubeEvil4 v0.98.1 (Beta)

> **Beta.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Savegames).

Changes since v0.98.0.

## Fixed

- Android 16: on builds whose app folder on the shared storage is unavailable (an Android bug), START GAME and the disc imports are no longer disabled; the app keeps its data in its internal storage instead.
- PSone demake: textures no longer slide along long surfaces such as beams while you walk, carpets no longer vanish in patches, fine textures flicker much less, surfaces close together (skirtings, fireplaces, Ashley's belt) no longer flicker, and blinking items no longer show through walls.
- 60 FPS: a fire on the ground no longer flickers while you look up or down.
- Instant save works again at the gondola before the castle (with its merchant and typewriter); only the ride itself stays locked.
- Crash in the castle after a long session when you change the weapon in the attache case (e.g. to the rifle in the lava room).
- Crash on some 64-bit phones when you go through a door (e.g. in the castle in chapter 4).
- Ada, HUNK, Krauser and Wesker in the story: kicks, suplexes and their own finishing moves no longer freeze the game, and Ganados react properly.

## New

- Ada, HUNK, Krauser and Wesker in the story can tell Ashley to wait or follow, as Leon does.

## Changed

- Graphics: the option "Vertex ring buffer (experimental)" no longer says "for Mali".

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames can still flicker now and then.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The sound can have a short gap now and then (shots, the ending movie on some devices).
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.98.1-beta.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.

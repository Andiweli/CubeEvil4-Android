# CubeEvil4 v0.96.3 (Beta)

> **Beta.** The whole game can be played from start to finish. Expect bugs and keep a copy of your
> saves (app menu → Management → Export Savegames).

Changes since v0.96.2.

## Fixed

- The Mercenaries: only one clock; the second one no longer appears after opening the inventory.
- The Mercenaries: time pick-ups read "30sec. extended!" and "You have 30sec." instead of "PTAS" and frames (900).
- The Mercenaries: time pick-ups play their own sounds instead of the money sound, as in the retail game.
- The Mercenaries: after a match or "Game Reset" the title screen no longer flashes up before the Mercenaries menu.
- The Mercenaries with retail discs: enemies come back as in the retail game (not while you are on a ladder; with European and Japanese discs they stop short of you, and on one map two more areas refill).
- The Mercenaries with retail discs, Waterworld: enemies are set as in the retail game (one more wave, the first enemies search for you instead of running at you).
- Merchant (European discs): the buy line starts at the left of the text box, so long lines (Spanish handcannon) fit, as in the European game.
- Village, iron door: after using the key the right message appears instead of "You got 10" (European discs).
- Kicked Ganados no longer fly through closed doors anywhere (before, the fix covered only the village Ganados of the story).
- Controller with your own button mapping: running or aiming held while the inventory closes works at once again.
- Japanese and Korean discs: the map in Assignment Ada and The Mercenaries opens and closes again (it stayed empty).
- Chicago Typewriter: infinite ammo from the start, as in the retail game (a Typewriter already in a save keeps its old levels).

## Known issues

- 60 FPS (experimental): big torch flames can still flicker now and then.
- Infinite health does not stop the instant deaths of some attacks (chainsaw, grabs, boulders).
- Cutscenes keep their letterbox with the picture format "4:3".
- The sound can have a short gap now and then (shots, the ending movie on some devices).
- Debug build's discs: Ashley can walk through the armor statues (the prototype's data has no collision there).

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.96.3-beta.apk` over an earlier version; discs, saves and settings stay.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.
